💡 Second Language Acquisition (SLA) Framework

Stephen Krashen's
5 Hypotheses of Language Acquisition

Understand the scientific theory behind why watching cartoons with context acquires language up to 5x faster than grammar drill memorization.

1

The Input Hypothesis (i+1)

We acquire language only when we understand messages slightly above our current level (i+1). Visual cues bridge the gap without requiring conscious dictionary lookups.

2

The Affective Filter Hypothesis

Anxiety, self-consciousness, and stress raise an invisible barrier that blocks language from reaching the brain's acquisition device. Cartoons lower the filter to near zero.

3

Acquisition vs. Learning

Acquisition is a subconscious, natural process identical to how children acquire their first tongue. Learning is conscious memorization of grammar rules which does not produce fluent speech.

4

The Natural Order Hypothesis

Grammatical structures are acquired in an innate predictable order, independent of the order in which they are taught in textbooks. Natural immersion respects this innate order.

5

The Monitor Hypothesis

Conscious learning only acts as an internal editor (monitor) to inspect self-produced speech. Over-monitoring causes hesitation, stuttering, and fear of speaking.
